# Monetization

No pricing or revenue model is approved.

## Options from the concept

- BYOK: the user supplies an API key and pays the AI provider.
- Managed AI usage: CowWorker charges for usage or credit packs.

The original 3% markup is an unvalidated idea, not a price commitment.
BYOK and managed credits require different billing and cost tracking.

## Open questions

- Which product features create enough value to charge for?
- Who pays for AI requests, synchronization, and document storage?
- What costs remain after provider charges, payment fees, and support?
- What free usage, limits, and refund rules are appropriate?

No payment integration exists. See [product scope](PRODUCT.md).
